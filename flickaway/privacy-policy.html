Flickaway Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 October 2026

In short: Flickaway has no accounts and doesn't send your personal data to the developer. Your game progress stays on your device. The app shows ads through Google AdMob, which may collect device data as described below, and in-app purchases are handled by Google Play.

This policy explains how the Flickaway mobile game ("the app", "we") handles information. It applies to the Android app published on Google Play under the package name com.ankurpohekar286.flickaway.

1. Information stored on your device

The app saves your game progress on your device only: your current level, the number of hints you have, whether you have purchased "Remove ads", your sound and vibration settings, and that you accepted our Terms of Service. This information isn't sent to us or to anyone else. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows banner, interstitial and optional rewarded video ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share:

  • your device's advertising ID and app set ID;
  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location;
  • information about your device and the app, such as model, operating system version and language;
  • interactions with ads, such as views and taps;
  • crash logs and diagnostics related to ad delivery.

We don't receive this information in a form that identifies you. Google's use of it is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices: In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, using Google's consent tool. Where available, you can change your choice at any time from Shop → Privacy settings in the app. On any Android device you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact menu varies by device).

3. In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

Purchases such as "Remove ads" and hint packs are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. The app only receives confirmation from Google Play of which product was bought, so it can unlock it, and restore it if you reinstall. Google's handling of purchase data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

4. What we don't do

  • We don't require an account, sign-in, name, email address or phone number.
  • We don't run our own servers, analytics or tracking.
  • We don't access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.
  • We don't sell your personal information.

5. Children

Flickaway is intended for users aged 13 and over and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app in a way that resulted in data being collected, contact us and we'll help address it.

6. Security and data retention

Data sent by the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google Play is encrypted in transit. Because we don't collect or store your data on our own systems, there is nothing for us to retain. Game progress stays on your device until you uninstall the app or clear its data.

7. Your rights and deleting data

To delete the game progress stored on your device, uninstall Flickaway or clear its storage in your phone's settings. To manage advertising data, use your device's ad settings as described in section 2, or visit My Ad Center. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights over your personal data; contact us and we'll help you exercise them.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
